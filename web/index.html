Key Metrics & Regulatory Definitions
AUPRC (Area Under Precision-Recall Curve) Gold Standard Metric

What it means: The ultimate quality score for fraud models. Standard "Accuracy" is dangerously misleading in banking because 98% of transactions are innocent. A lazy model that marks everything licit has 98% accuracy but catches zero fraud!

Why it matters: AUPRC evaluates how well the model finds the needle in the haystack without flooding investigators with false alarms. Our proposed model achieves 0.5267 AUPRC (+121% over static baseline).
Recall (Fraud Catch Rate) Loss Prevention

What it means: The percentage of actual criminal laundering transactions the system successfully intercepts out of all real fraud in circulation: Caught Fraud / Total Actual Fraud.

Why it matters: Low recall means criminals slip through. Static models drop to 0.0% recall at Step 43. Our in-context defense maintains 72.0% post-shock recall, stopping 72 out of 100 illicit transfers.
Precision (Alert Accuracy) Investigation Efficiency

What it means: When the alarm sounds and flags a transaction, how often is it a real criminal rather than an innocent business or customer? True Fraud / All Flagged Alerts.

Why it matters: Low precision overwhelms bank compliance staff with false alarms ( investigation cost each). High precision keeps investigators focused on genuine financial crime.
Concept Drift Evolving Threats

What it means: Criminal syndicates changing their behavior over time. A model trained on January's laundering patterns goes blind in March when cartels switch to multi-hop peel chains and mixers.

The Banking Trap: Banks cannot retrain monthly because Federal Reserve SR 11-7 model-risk approval takes ~16 weeks per cycle, leaving institutions exposed to stale models.
PSI (Population Stability Index) Drift Smoke Detector

What it means: An industry-standard mathematical score measuring how much live transaction patterns have shifted compared to the baseline training dataset.

Regulatory Rule: PSI < 0.10 = Stable; 0.10–0.25 = Moderate shift; PSI > 0.25 = Severe Crisis. At Step 43, graph PSI reached 0.81, signaling an urgent evasion shock.
Tree-SHAP (Explainable AI) Regulatory Audit Trail

What it means: An adverse-action receipt showing the exact mathematical reasons why a specific transaction was blocked or flagged, replacing opaque "black-box" decisions.

Compliance Value: Gives compliance officers documented evidence (e.g. "+38% risk from 2-hop fund splits, +25% from abnormal fee ratio") required for official FinCEN SAR filings.
